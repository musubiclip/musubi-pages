# Instagram growth research for 10 niche clip pages (researched 2026-10-22)

Marks: **[official]** = Instagram/Meta page, docs, or Mosseri on record (quoted by press when noted). **[press]** = press reporting or third party data. **[folklore]** = creator, vendor or agency claim, unverified.

## 1. Originality, reposts, clips

- **[official] Apr 30 2026: the aggregator rule now covers every format.** It started with Reels in 2024 and now covers photos and carousels too. An account stays recommendable only when "most of your recently posted reels, photos, and carousels are considered original in a 30-day period", counted on a rolling basis. Followers still see everything, so the penalty only cuts reach to non-followers. Instagram says 75% of US recommendations now come from original posts. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram (2026-04-30); https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines/ (current, © 2026)
- **[official] Burnt-in subtitles are explicitly not a material edit.** The 2026 post lists the edits that do not count: "just adding a border, watermark, subtitles, or a credit in the captions". The edits that do count are "unique text on the content itself that provides more context than just describing what's happening", "creative graphics that offer new information", and Remix. The guidelines add narration, a storyline, and "a perspective, joke, or context that wasn't there before". The test is: "if someone could remove your contribution… and the content would virtually be the same", it is not original. "Popular clips" are named as third party material that is allowed only when it is enhanced. Same sources as above.
- **[official] A different caption or a credit line does not count.** Speed changes and screenshots that show the creator's name do not count either. https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines/ ; https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/ (2026-04-30)
- **[official, via press] Mosseri, Apr 2026:** "If most of what you post to Instagram is someone else's content, your account is no longer going to be recommendable." He suggested remixing with your own green screen, words on top or commentary, and using built-in reposts or collabs to credit the original poster. https://www.webpronews.com/instagrams-aggregator-purge-original-creators-gain-ground-as-repost-empires-crumble/ (2026-05-02); https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/ (2026-04-30)
- **[official] 2024 rules that still apply:**
  - When identical content exists, only the original is recommended. This applies when the original is "relatively new" and audio plus visual signals confirm the match. Copies get a label that links to the original creator.
  - A memed, parodied, voiced over or reaction remixed piece is not replaced.
  - To be eligible for recommendations, a reel must have "no visible watermarks".
  - Aggregators are defined as accounts that "repeatedly post content… that they didn't create or enhance in a material way".
  - Excluded: "a set of publishers we've identified who have licensing agreements and/or explicit permissions". That is a list Instagram keeps; you cannot sign up for it yourself.

  https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality (2024-04-30)
- **[press] The 2024 threshold was "10 or more times in the last 30 days".** The account regained eligibility after 30 days without reposting. The 2026 wording replaced this with "most posts in a rolling 30 days". https://campaignme.com/instagram-revamps-algorithm-for-original-and-emerging-content-creators/ (2024-05-06)
- **[official] Permission does not make a clip original.** The originality rules are "separate from our intellectual property policies". "You are responsible for obtaining any necessary permissions." Permission keeps you safe on copyright but does not restore recommendations. https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines/
- **[official] Watermarked or already posted reels get less reach.** In the 2023 ranking explainer, "low-resolution or watermarked reels" and reels "already posted on Instagram" are made less visible. https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained (2023-05-31)
- **[official] Recommendation tips from 2022 to 2023:** vertical 9:16 and high resolution, "no borders", little text covering the screen, 3 minutes or less, a hook in the first 3 seconds, no watermarks. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators (2022-08-30, updated 2023-04-25)
- **[official] Meta's Facebook crackdown (Jul 14 2025) covers Facebook only.** The post does not say it applies to Instagram.
  - It reads: "simply stitching together clips or adding your watermark does not qualify as meaningful enhancement."
  - It also says to "avoid visible third party watermarks and content visibly recycled from other apps".
  - Penalties are lost monetization and "reduced distribution on everything they share".
  - Its caption tips: no links, minimal capitalization, at most 5 hashtags.

  This matters if pages cross-post to Facebook. https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content (2025-07-14)
- **[press] Meta Content Protection (Nov 2025) lets enrolled original creators act on copies across Facebook and Instagram.** They can track copies, add an "Original by" attribution, or block distribution of copies. https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/11/18/facebook-content-protection-copycat-action-meta/ (2025-11-18)
- **[press] NPR reports that platforms are cracking down on clip pages.** The target is pages "where countless duplicates of the same moment are spread", because they look spammy or bot-like. https://text.npr.org/nx-s1-5794670 (2026-05-12)

## 2. "Shadowban", Account Status, eligibility

- **[official] Instagram's answer to "shadowban" is recommendation eligibility.** Instagram says the term "isn't one shared definition". It points to Account Status as "the best way to understand if your account and content is eligible to be recommended". Repeated violations make "none of their posts… eligible for recommendation for a period of time". https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained (2023-05-31)
- **[official] Account Status is for professional accounts.** It shows whether content can be recommended to non-followers in Explore, Reels and Feed recommendations, plus eligibility in Search and Suggested Accounts. It shows sample offending posts, and the profile photo or bio alone can cause ineligibility. You can remove the content or request a review there. Path: Settings → Account Status. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators (2022, updated 2023-04-25)
- **[official] What the Recommendations Guidelines rule out.** This content is allowed on Instagram but not recommended:
  - Safety: self-harm, violence such as fighting, sexually suggestive content, regulated products.
  - Health and finance: cosmetic procedures, "miracle cures", misleading business models such as payday loans.
  - Political content.
  - Clickbait and engagement bait.
  - Low quality repurposed or unoriginal content.
  - False or misleading content.

  https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality (2024-04-30)
  For the Finance, Beauty and Fitness pages: exaggerated health claims, cosmetic procedure content and "misleading business models" are recommendation risks.
- **[official] How long it lasts.** Guideline strikes last "for a period of time" with no number given. Originality ineligibility lifts when most posts in the rolling 30-day window are original. You can appeal from Account Status. Sources: above.
- **[folklore] Reviews take 24 to 72 hours, and recovery takes 1 to 4 weeks.** These come from vendor blogs, not Instagram. https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-account-health ; https://intellicoach.ai/blog/instagram-not-eligible-for-recommendations-non-followers
- **[press] Organic reach is down for everyone, and that is not a shadowban.** Reels reach fell 35% year on year (Metricool 2026, about 1.06M accounts) and the average reach rate fell 12% (Socialinsider). https://www.sotrender.com/blog/2026/08/instagram-reach-is-down-what-the-data-actually-says/ (2026-08-11)

## 3. What grows a new Reels page (official vs folklore)

- **[official, via press] The top signals are watch time, likes and sends** (Mosseri, Jan 2025). Watch three numbers: average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. "Sends are slightly more important for unconnected content", meaning non-followers, which is exactly how a new page grows. Comments were not named. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/ (2025-01-22)
- **[official, via press] Mosseri's Sept 2026 ranking series named four principles:**
  - Originality: "from the original creator, as opposed to re-posters".
  - Recency: newer posts get more exposure.
  - Breaking content: posts from small public creators are tested with non-followers.
  - Bigger models.

  He suggested using Trial Reels to test, and said to post as much as you can without burning out. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/ (2026-09-24)
- **[official] Every eligible reel is first shown to a small audience, then expands in stages.** A 2024 change gave smaller creators more distribution. https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality (2024-04-30)
- **[official] Trial Reels** (launched 2024-12-10):
  - Shown to non-followers only, kept off the grid.
  - Results arrive after about 24 hours.
  - Optional auto-share to followers if views in the first 72 hours are good.

  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels
- **[official] The publishing API supports Trial Reels.** Use `trial_params.graduation_strategy` set to `MANUAL` or `SS_PERFORMANCE`. https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing (current)
- **[folklore] Trial Reels may need around 1,000 followers.** Instagram publishes no threshold. https://info.bundle.social/api-reference/platforms/instagram
- **[official] Other API facts:**
  - Limit of 100 API-published posts per 24 hours per account.
  - The `collaborators` field takes up to 3 usernames on reels.
  - There is no way to attach licensed or trending music. `audio_name` only names your original audio.

  https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media
- **[official, via press] Mosseri on scheduled posts:** they "will not affect your reach". Vendor sourced, but consistent across sources. https://planoly.com/blog/does-scheduling-your-posts-affect-your-post-reach
- **[official, via press] Reels length.**
  - Jan 2025: the Reels limit rose to 3 minutes, and Instagram said reels up to 3 minutes get equal recommendation priority. https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-give-equal-recommendation-to-reels-3-minutes-in-length/ (2025-01-22)
  - [press, data] Median views peak at 45 to 60 seconds (10,374). Views are flat from 30 seconds to 3 minutes and halve past 180 seconds. Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to non-followers. Dataset: 9.77M brand Reels, H1 2026. https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/ (2026-07-14, updated 2026-10-20)
- **[official, via press] Hashtags.**
  - Capped at 5 per post or reel, rolled out gradually.
  - Mosseri says hashtags "don't increase your reach" but help say what a post is about. Avoid generic tags like #reels and #explore.

  https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ (2025-12-18)
- **[press] Search.**
  - Public posts and reels from professional accounts (18+) can be indexed by Google and Bing since Jul 10 2025, so keyword captions now have SEO value. https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/ (2025-07)
  - Instagram is prioritising keywords in captions, profile and alt text for in-app search. https://openmoves.com/blog/do-hashtags-still-matter/ (citing a Business Insider interview from Apr 2025)
- **[official] Keywords in the handle, name, bio and captions; hashtags go in the caption, not comments.** https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators (2023)
- **[official, via press] Posting frequency.** Mosseri: "Prioritize quality over quantity, but in general, the more you post, the more people you'll reach." He also says to pick a sustainable cadence. https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/
- **[press, data] Buffer study** (2M posts, Aug 2025): monthly follower growth was +0.12% at 1 to 2 posts a week, +0.26% at 3 to 5, +0.44% at 6 to 9, and +0.66% at 10 or more, with diminishing returns per post. Growth dipped after a week with no posts. https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/
- **[official, via press] Warming up a new account does not affect reach.** Mosseri, Aug 2025: "what you decide to consume is not going to affect who you reach with your content." He said Edits gives "a little bit" of temporary reach boost, and that passing content through Edits just to get it can be detected. https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-encourages-use-edits-app-short-term-boost-9647455 (2025-08-11)
- **[official, via press] Mosseri, Sept 2026: editing a post after publishing "won't hurt your reach".** https://www.unum.la/blog/adam-mosseri-shuts-down-instagram-engagement-myth-edits-do-boost-reach (2026-09-25)
- **[press] Audio.** A Mosseri AMA, relayed secondhand, says there is no penalty for popular audio and no evidence that original audio gets a boost. https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/trending-audio-instagram-reach/ (2026)
- **[official] Your Algorithm** lets viewers see and edit the topics behind their Reels recommendations (US launch Dec 2025, later expanded to Explore and the main feed). A clear single niche per page fits this. https://searchengineland.com/instagram-your-algorithm-465987
- **[folklore]** These are widespread but not stated by Instagram:
  - Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  - A text hook that lands in about 1.7 seconds.
  - Bold, word by word captions in the Hormozi style.
  - Warm a new account for 5 to 7 days before posting (https://www.ssemble.com/blog/account-warmup-guide-2026). This contradicts Mosseri.
  - Day one posting gets "suppressed".
  - The "10 reposts" number for 2026.
  - Reach collapses of 60 to 80% for aggregators.

## 4. Clip page case studies and playbooks (2025 to 2026)

- **[press] NPR:** a Belgian clipper (Emrah Bayraktar) claims 6 clip pages, all past 100K followers, about 1.1M in total, built over 3+ years. He started by cutting long influencer interviews into Instagram snippets. Clippers lean on provocative moments, music and timely news, and the same clip goes to several platforms. Per view campaign pay is $0.50 to $25 per 1,000 views. https://text.npr.org/nx-s1-5794670 (2026-05-12)
- **[press] Net Influencer, "Clipping 101":**
  - Most clippers run 1 or 2 pages; earners already have audiences.
  - "Hundreds of versions of the same footage" circulate within a day.
  - Unlicensed footage is a "ticking time bomb"; serious agencies write rights into clipper terms.
  - "A podcast moment cut to thirty seconds" is the standard unit.

  https://www.netinfluencer.com/clipping-101-inside-the-clipping-economy/ (2026-09-26)
- **[folklore, vendor] Overlap and Thursday Labs:** a new podcast clips account posted 20 to 30 reels a day. Any clip passing 5,000 views was reposted as a collab with the show account (about 40K followers) and the host (about 25K). They claim faster learning but publish no follower numbers. https://www.overlap.ai/case-studies/thursday-labs-case-study (2025-12-14)
- **[folklore] Clip niche guides** say to pick under-clipped sources and be first to post a moment. https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/best-niches-clip-channels-2026.html (2026)

## Plan vs research: what is wrong or risky

1. **A hook plus burnt-in captions plus a page style probably does not count as original.** Subtitles and credits are explicitly listed as not enough (2026-04-30). The hook counts only if it adds context, not description. Each clip needs a removable-contribution test pass: on-screen commentary or context cards, own graphics or explainers, voiceover or narration, a green screen reaction, or a storyline across clips. Otherwise all 10 pages become aggregators: no non-follower reach at all, so no growth.
2. **Owner permission does not restore recommendations.** The licensing exemption covers only publishers Instagram has identified. Permission protects against copyright, not ranking.
3. **Duplicate replacement.** If the podcast's own Instagram account, or other clippers, posted the same moment first, only the original is recommended. Pick moments not yet clipped, post soon after the episode drops, or use the source's IG account as a collaborator (the API allows up to 3), which also signals the relationship. Never post the same clip on two of your own pages.
4. **The letterboxed 16:9 template** (bars or blur above and below) risks the "no borders" and "borders are low effort" rules. Reframe to full-bleed 9:16.
5. **Random times across 24 hours.** Recency is a stated ranking principle, so 3 am IST posts start cold. Randomize inside each niche's active window, which Insights shows.
6. **No watermarks of any kind:** YouTube UI, logos, OpusClip or CapCut marks, the source channel's on-screen bug if possible.
7. **Copyright inside the source:** music, film or TV clips, news footage. Rights Manager or Content Protection matches lead to takedowns, and repeat infringement can disable the account. Keep written licences and ask owners not to block the pages.
8. **Hashtags:** 5 maximum, specific, for search not reach. Put keywords in the caption and on-screen text instead.
9. **Volume:** 1 to 3 a day is fine (Mosseri: more posts, more reach). Use Trial Reels through the API (`SS_PERFORMANCE`) to test hooks once a page is eligible.
10. **Check Account Status on all 10 pages weekly**, especially Finance, Beauty and Fitness, where health and financial claims make content non-recommendable.
